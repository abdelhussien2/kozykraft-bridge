Privacy Policy

Last updated May 13, 2026

The Loaf Report ("we," "us," or "our") publishes editorial content and product recommendations related to home baking and sourdough. This policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

When you visit, we automatically collect:

  • Your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device information
  • Pages you view, links you click, and time spent on the site
  • Referring URLs and search terms that brought you here
  • Approximate geographic location based on IP

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by emailing us).

Cookies & Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to recognize your browser, understand site usage, and improve user experience. We also use third-party analytics and advertising pixels — including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel — which collects information about your visit to deliver more relevant advertising on Meta platforms.

You can opt out of Meta's advertising personalization at facebook.com/settings?tab=ads, or clear cookies and use browser extensions that block tracking pixels.

Sponsored Content

The Loaf Report publishes sponsored editorial content. Some articles — including our coverage of KozyKraft sourdough starter products — are sponsored advertising. We may receive compensation when readers click through to product listings and make purchases. This is disclosed at the top of each sponsored article per FTC guidelines.

How Your Information Is Used

  • To operate, maintain, and improve the website
  • To analyze traffic patterns and user behavior
  • To deliver relevant advertising on Meta, Amazon, and other third-party platforms
  • To respond to inquiries you send us

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share aggregated, non-identifiable usage information with our advertisers and partners. Certain visitor data is shared with Meta and Amazon when their pixels are present, in accordance with their respective policies.

Third-Party Links

Our articles link to third-party websites, including Amazon.com. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. Read their privacy policies before providing personal information.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. Contact us at the email below to make a request.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The date at the top reflects the most recent revision.
