Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 13, 2026

The Loaf Report ("we," "us," or "our") publishes editorial content and product recommendations related to home baking and sourdough. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and disclose information when you visit our website.

Information We Collect

When you visit our site, we automatically collect certain information about your device and browsing activity, including:

— Your IP address, browser type, operating system, and device information
— Pages you view, links you click, and time spent on the site
— Referring URLs and search terms that brought you to our site
— Geographic location (approximate, based on IP)

We do not directly collect personally identifiable information unless you voluntarily provide it (for example, by emailing us).

Cookies and Tracking Technologies

We use cookies and similar technologies to recognize your browser, understand site usage, and improve user experience. We also use third-party analytics and advertising pixels, including the Meta (Facebook) Pixel, which collects information about your visit to deliver more relevant advertising on Meta platforms.

You can opt out of Meta's advertising personalization by adjusting your settings at facebook.com/settings?tab=ads. You can also clear cookies or use browser extensions that block tracking pixels.

Sponsored Content Disclosure

The Loaf Report publishes sponsored editorial content. Some articles on this site, including our coverage of KozyKraft sourdough starter products, are sponsored advertising content. We may receive compensation from advertisers when readers click through to product listings and make purchases. This is disclosed at the top of each sponsored article in accordance with FTC guidelines.

How Your Information Is Used

The information we collect is used to:

— Operate, maintain, and improve our website
— Analyze traffic patterns and user behavior
— Deliver relevant advertising on third-party platforms (Meta, Amazon, and others)
— Respond to inquiries you send us

Information Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. We may share aggregated, non-identifiable usage information with our advertisers and partners. We share certain visitor data with Meta and Amazon when their pixels are present on our pages, in accordance with their respective policies.

Third-Party Links

Our articles contain links to third-party websites, including Amazon.com. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

Your Rights

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have rights to access, correct, or delete personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at the email address below.

Children's Privacy

Our site is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can remove it.
