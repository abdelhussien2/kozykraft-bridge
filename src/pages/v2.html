We make KozyKraft, and the question we get most is what actually happens in the jar. So here's the week, day by day, with what's normal and what isn't.

Day 1: Wake it up.

Empty the whole packet into the jar, stir in 1.5 tablespoons of filtered water, and leave it for an hour. Then add 1 tablespoon of flour, stir it to a thick paste, and rest the lid loosely on top. Mark the level with the rubber band.

Day 2: Nothing yet. That's normal.

Feed it 2 tablespoons of flour and 1.5 tablespoons of water. No bubbles, no rise. The pouch says this is expected, so keep going. This is the day most people quit.

Day 3: First bubbles.

Same feed. Small bubbles on the surface and a faint sour smell. It's alive.

Day 4: The big feed, and a bigger jar.

Feed it a third of a cup of flour and a quarter cup of water. It will need a bigger jar after this one.

Day 5: It doubled.

Feed it in the morning; by afternoon it should be well past the rubber band, domed, and full of bubbles. That's the ready signal.

Day 6: First loaf.

Use 100 grams of starter in a basic Dutch oven loaf.

Day 7: Verdict.

It works, and the part that matters is day 2. If you know a quiet day is normal, you don't throw it out. If you don't, you do. Everything you need to know is printed on the pouch, and there's a day-7 guarantee if yours doesn't bubble.
